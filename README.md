# Birds Aren't Real

A Three.js game where you play PGN-0451, a surveillance pigeon on a rainy night at Broadway & Spring St in SoHo, Manhattan.

Open `index.html` in a modern browser (it loads three.js r160 from jsDelivr and fonts from Google Fonts).

## What's in it

- **Wet Manhattan street corner**: planar reflections on the asphalt, puddles with rain ripples, oil slicks with thin-film color, crosswalks and lane markings, steam from a striped Con Ed stack and manholes, lit storefronts, neon, fire escapes, water towers, a sidewalk shed, a subway entrance, traffic signals with walk/don't-walk heads.
- **Traffic and people**: taxis and cars that obey the signal cycle, pedestrians with umbrellas who wait for the walk sign.
- **Two views**: third person, and **BirdCam** (first-person eye camera with fisheye, lens raindrops, REC timestamp, GPS, subject tagging).
- **Other units**: Gus, Dolores, Biscuit, Static, Marlene, Commander Featherstone and Kevin, each with branching dialogue, plus an ambient flock that scatters when people walk through.
- **A shift of missions**: tag civilians, recharge on streetlight heads, keep watch on the woman in the yellow raincoat, investigate Kevin, collect bread intel.

## Controls

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Mouse | Look and steer (click to lock the pointer) |
| W A S D | Walk on the ground, fly in the air |
| Space | Take off, climb |
| Shift | Dive; peck when on the ground |
| Q | Burst speed |
| C | Toggle BirdCam |
| F / click | Tag the subject in the reticle (BirdCam) |
| Wheel | Zoom (BirdCam) or camera distance |
| E | Talk to a nearby unit |
| G | Coo |
| H | Field manual / pause |
| M | Mute |

On touch screens there is a stick plus Flap, Dive, Cam, Tag, Talk and Coo buttons. Graphics quality (High/Low) can be switched from the title screen or the field manual, and resolution adapts automatically to keep the frame rate up.
